HISTORY AND ESTABLISHMENT

The fund was established through a generous benefaction from Lord Nuffield in
1937. It was formerly known as the Oxford and District Hospitals Improvement
and Development Fund.

PURPOSE (every grant application must meet this test, verbatim as stated by
the Fund)

"The raising of the standard of the hospital or hospitals concerned in order
to assist the development and carrying on of the Oxford University Medical
School."

HOW IT OPERATES

The Nuffield Oxford Hospitals Fund gives grants to a total of around £450,000
every year. Applications operate on an annual cycle, with submissions due by
the end of January each year, reviewed by Trustees meeting in May or June.
Recent grants have typically been in the £10,000 to £40,000 range; the
Trustees do not allocate grants for less than £5,000.

WHAT IT FUNDS

NOHF funds items of equipment and larger projects across multiple hospital
specialties, in direct support of the Oxford University Medical School's
training of doctors in Oxfordshire. Past grants have supported: upgrades to
educational facilities and medical student spaces; educational films and
communication training; building extensions; medical equipment (manikins,
microscopes); simulator training facilities; healthcare library resources
(including desktops and laptops); seminar room and hybrid audio-visual
teaching equipment; and publication of paediatric surgical oncology
guidebooks.
