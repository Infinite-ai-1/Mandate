PURPOSE

The British Thoracic Society exists to improve standards of care for people
who have respiratory diseases and to support and develop those who provide
that care. It is a registered charity and a company limited by guarantee,
with activities covering all of the UK. BTS represents over 5,000 respiratory
specialists, from consultants and trainees to nurses, scientists, pharmacists
and allied health professionals.

VISION

Better lung health for all.

MISSION

- To influence the provision of the optimum respiratory workforce and
  research-centred services that promote sustainable solutions and reduce
  health inequalities.
- To educate professionals to advance knowledge and share learning in the
  prevention, diagnosis and treatment of lung disease.

INVESTMENT POLICY
(Position Statement: Sustainability and the Environment, section 30, "Roles
and Responsibilities - what organisations can do")

30.5 The BTS investment policy commits to no investment in the tobacco
industry, arms trade or fossil fuel industries. The BTS Board will continue
to review the impact of investments and explore opportunities to actively
support projects which deliver benefits to respiratory patients.

RELATED POSITIONS, FOR CONTEXT (same Position Statement)

Context and background (section 1):
1.1 The British Thoracic Society (BTS) is committed to highlighting the
impact of climate change on those living with respiratory disease. BTS
aspires to be a leading voice in sustainable healthcare and inspire our
members to take action and contribute to a healthy and just future in which
everyone can thrive.

On fossil fuels (section 5.3): BTS will work through UKHACC [UK Health
Alliance on Climate Change] to highlight scientific and health evidence that
can influence policy on fossil fuel non-proliferation, prioritisation of
plant-based and sustainably sourced food, and mitigation and adaptation to
the planetary crisis.

On tobacco (section 6, "Prevention - tobacco dependence"):
6.1 The production of cigarettes has direct environmental impacts, including
deforestation, air and water pollution, and an estimated carbon footprint of
80 million tonnes of CO2 per year.
6.2 BTS supports all actions to reduce smoking prevalence year on year,
including those outlined in the Khan Review, with the ultimate goal of
eliminating tobacco-related lung disease in the UK.

NOTE ON FRACKING: the source Position Statement commits BTS against the
broader category of "fossil fuel industries" rather than naming fracking
specifically; fracking (shale gas extraction) falls within that exclusion.
